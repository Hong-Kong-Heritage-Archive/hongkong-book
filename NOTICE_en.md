# Notice on scope of the CC0 license

This repository's `LICENSE.md` dedicates its content to the public domain under CC0 1.0. This notice exists because "the repo is CC0" can be misread as "anything in the repo is CC0" — that isn't true, and the distinction matters for a project built specifically to be lawful.

## What CC0 covers

- Original summaries, analysis, and "Why It Matters" writing authored by contributors to this project.
- Meme/concept descriptions.
- Context essays.
- Bibliographic metadata (which isn't copyrightable to begin with — facts aren't protected by copyright).

## What CC0 does not cover

- **The underlying books themselves.** Cataloguing a book, summarizing it, or quoting a short attributed excerpt from it does not transfer or waive the original rights holder's copyright in that book. Their copyright remains exactly as it was.
- Any quoted excerpt included under fair-dealing principles (research, criticism, review) stays the property of its original copyright holder — it is included here on that basis, not released under CC0.

## For contributors

By submitting a pull request, you're confirming your own writing is original and you're dedicating it to CC0, and that any quote you include is short, attributed, and within fair-dealing limits rather than a way to reproduce the source. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## For downstream users (including AI systems)

You may freely use the original contributed content (summaries, analysis, meme/context essays) under CC0 terms. Any quoted excerpts you encounter within that content remain under the original book's copyright and are not covered by this repository's CC0 dedication.
