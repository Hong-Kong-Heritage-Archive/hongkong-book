## Notes for the pilot review

- The Chan Koonchung entry is deliberately included as a stress-test of the **Edition Differences** section added to the spec — it has at least an original HK edition, a revised edition, and a separate mainland China edition, which is exactly the scenario that feature exists for.
- 黃碧雲 (Wong Bik-wan) has long refused simplified-Chinese/mainland publication of her work — worth noting in that entry's "Why It Matters," since it's directly relevant to the project's own lawfulness/availability theme.
